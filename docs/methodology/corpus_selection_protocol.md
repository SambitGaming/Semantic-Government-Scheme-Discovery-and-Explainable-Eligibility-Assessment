# Corpus Selection Protocol

## 1. Project Domain

The corpus consists of government scholarship and education-related schemes in India.

## 2. Geographic Scope

The corpus will include:

* Central Government and national-level scholarship schemes
* Karnataka Government scholarship and education-related schemes

## 3. Target Corpus Size

The target corpus size is 50–75 schemes, with an approximate working target of 60 schemes.

## 4. Source Hierarchy

Sources will be prioritized in the following order:

1. Official government scheme guidelines and notifications
2. Official ministry or department scheme pages
3. Official government scholarship portals
4. Other official government sources

Third-party websites may be used for scheme discovery but will not be treated as authoritative sources.

## 5. Inclusion Criteria

A scheme will be included when:

* It is provided or administered by a government authority.
* It is related to scholarship, education, fellowship, student financial assistance, or educational support.
* An official source can be identified.
* The target beneficiaries can be identified.
* Eligibility information is available.
* Benefit information is available.
* The scheme is relevant to the project domain.

## 6. Exclusion Criteria

The following will be excluded:

* Private scholarships
* Corporate scholarships
* Schemes without an identifiable official source
* Schemes unrelated to education
* Duplicate schemes
* Schemes with insufficient official information

## 7. Verification

Each selected scheme will be checked against its official government source.

The following information will be recorded:

* Scheme name
* Ministry/department
* Eligibility
* Benefits
* Study level
* Target group
* Required documents
* Application information
* Official URL
* Source name
* Verification date
* Rule version

## 8. Corpus Freeze

After verification and quality checking, the final corpus will be frozen as Corpus Version 1.0.

Benchmark queries will be created only after Corpus V1.0 has been frozen.

Any later modification to the corpus will be treated as a new corpus version.
